Flotation Reagent
Removes stubborn stains

Flotation reagents are used in the <link;immersive_metallurgy:flotation_cell;Flotation Cell;multiblock>. They modify the surface properties of minerals, improving or reducing their floatability so that the slurry's properties and froth stability become more favorable for mineral separation.<br>
A good flotation reagent should have good §3frothing ability§r and §5collecting ability§r.<br>
The better the frothing ability, the higher the flotation efficiency and the shorter the time a recipe takes.<br>
The better the collecting ability, the higher the flotation yield — the higher the conversion rate from Processed Slurry to Concentrate Slurry.
<np>

<&plantoil>
§lSeed Oil§r
Flotation efficiency: 800mB/s
Flotation yield: 0.8 <br>
Seed oil can serve as a cheap flotation reagent, but it is rather inefficient: it can only adsorb a small amount of ore, so it is not recommended for long-term use.<br>
<np>

<&terpenic_oil>
§lTerpenic Oil§r
Flotation efficiency: 2000mB/s
Flotation yield: 1.0 <br>
Terpenic oil is an efficient and environmentally friendly flotation frother. Its frothing performance is excellent and can greatly improve flotation efficiency.<br>
Terpenic oil also has a weak collecting ability, so it can be used on its own.<br>
