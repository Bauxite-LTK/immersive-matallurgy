Metallurgy Blast Furnace
How Steel Is Made
<&multiblock>
The Metallurgy Blast Furnace is a machine dedicated to smelting pig iron, suitable for producing steel in large batches.
<np>

<&elite_blast_furnace0>
§lBlast Furnace Charge§r
The Metallurgy Blast Furnace accepts §1charge§r from the top and can store up to four stacks of §1charge§r inside the furnace body.<br>
The charge is the raw material for steelmaking; it can be crafted from §1Iron Concentrate Pellets§r and §1Coke Powder§r.
<np>

<&elite_blast_furnace1>
§lHot Blast Preheating§r
Both sides of the furnace need a continuous supply of §1Hot Air§r for heating. Four <link;immersive_metallurgy:hot_air_furnace;Gas Hot Air Furnace;multiblock>s running at full speed are required to reach and maintain the maximum temperature (1600°C).<br>
A standard charge requires at least §c1500°C§r to be smelted.
<np>

<&elite_blast_furnace2>
§lSmelting Pig Iron§r
Smelting the charge produces §1Molten Pig Iron§r, §3Blast Furnace Gas§r and slag.
§1Molten Pig Iron§r is output from the lower front of the furnace, §3Blast Furnace Gas§r from the top, and slag from the lower rear.<br>
1x charge --(5t)--> 100mB §1Molten Pig Iron§r
+ 300mB §3Blast Furnace Gas§r + 1x slag<br>
§3Blast Furnace Gas§r is a type of fuel gas and can be used as fuel for the <link;immersive_metallurgy:hot_air_furnace;Gas Hot Air Furnace;multiblock>.
