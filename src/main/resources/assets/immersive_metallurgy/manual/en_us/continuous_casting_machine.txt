<&multiblock>
The Continuous Casting Machine is a machine dedicated to continuously casting metal ingots, suitable for cooling molten metal in large batches.
<np>

<&continuous_casting_machine0>
§lContinuous Casting§r
The Continuous Casting Machine accepts §1molten metal§r from the upper port on one side.
As long as §2cooling water§r and §2cutting fuel§r are sufficient, it can continuously cast the §1molten metal§r into §1metal ingots§r.<br>
800mB molten metal --(40t)--> 8x metal ingots
<np>

<&continuous_casting_machine1>
§lCooling and Cutting§r
During casting, the Continuous Casting Machine continuously consumes §2cooling water§r and §2cutting fuel§r; see <link;immersive_metallurgy:gas_fuel;Gas;start> for details.<br>
Cooling water consumption rate: 50mB/t
Cutting fuel consumption rate: 1 Fuel/t<br>
If cooling water or fuel runs out during casting, the machine stops working and sounds an alert.
