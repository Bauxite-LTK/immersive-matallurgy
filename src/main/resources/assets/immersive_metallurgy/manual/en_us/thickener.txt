The Thickener is a solid-liquid separation device based on gravity sedimentation. It concentrates the mineral particles in the concentrate slurry and produces smeltable solids.

§lSedimentation and Thickening§r

Unlike the Flotation Cell, the Thickener only discharges its overflow actively when the total liquid inside reaches §2128000mB§r, though the overflow can also be pumped out directly.
