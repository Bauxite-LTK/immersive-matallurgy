<&multiblock>
The Ball Mill is a large machine that grinds materials into ultra-fine powder. It accepts coarse-grained powder as input.
<np>

<&ball_mill0>
The Ball Mill has §28 processing threads§r. Feed §1coarse powder§r into the middle port on one side and §1Water§r into the lower port; §1ore powder§r is output from the middle port on the other side, and §1Slurry§r from the lower port.<br>
The Ball Mill has two types of recipes: §2dry grinding§r and §2wet grinding§r:<br>
§lDry grinding§r grinds the ore directly into §1ore powder§r for smelting;<br>
§lWet grinding§r adds §1Water§r during grinding to obtain §1Slurry§r, which has greater yield potential.
